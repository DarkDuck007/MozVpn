This policy exists for one reason: so that users can trust that anything called "Moz VPN" comes from this project, and is not a modified, repackaged or paid copy that may be outdated, broken or malicious.

## Always allowed (no permission needed)

- **Talking about the project.** Articles, reviews, tutorials, videos and discussions that use the name to refer to Moz VPN.
- **Accurate statements of origin or compatibility.** For example: "based on Moz VPN", "a fork of Moz VPN", "compatible with Moz VPN servers". The name must not be the name of your product, and must not be more prominent than your own product's name.
- **Redistributing unmodified official releases**, free of charge, with the name and logo intact, as long as you link to the official repository: <https://github.com/DarkDuck007/MozVpn>

- **Distributing a modified version under the Marks.** Any build that changes features, adds or removes functionality, adds advertising, tracking or analytics, or changes server endpoints must use a different name.

- **Using a name, logo or domain name that is confusingly similar to the Marks**, or that suggests your product is official, endorsed by, or affiliated with this project.
- **Using the Marks in a way that disparages the project or misleads users.**

## Making a fork

You are welcome to fork Moz VPN; that is what the AGPL is for. To publish your fork, you must **rebrand it**:

1. Choose a different name that is not confusingly similar to "Moz VPN" or "MozVPN".
2. Replace the logos, icons, screenshots and store graphics.
3. Change the application IDs (for example `com.duckyvpn.mozvpn`) and package names.
4. Do not present your fork as an official, "pro", "premium" or "unlocked" version of Moz VPN.

You may say your fork is "based on Moz VPN", and you must still follow the AGPL, including providing the complete source code of your version.

If you want to do something this policy does not allow, open an issue at <https://github.com/DarkDuck007/MozVpn/issues> describing what you have in mind. Permission is granted only in writing and only for the specific use described.

## Reporting misuse

If you find a copy of Moz VPN being sold, or a modified version published under the Moz VPN name (for example in an app store), please report it by opening an issue at <https://github.com/DarkDuck007/MozVpn/issues> with a link to the listing.
